PRIVACY POLICY · LAST UPDATED 17 NOVEMBER 2026

Your photos stay yours.

Threadlook helps you find fashion products inspired by a photo. This policy explains what the app stores on your device and what is sent when you run a visual search.

Information on your device

Threadlook does not require an account. The app stores your onboarding choice, favorites, recent searches, search usage, and the current search session on your device. A recent search can include an image URI, the selected crop, the date, and product results.

You can clear recent searches from Settings and remove favorites in the Favorites screen. This removes Threadlook’s saved app records; it does not delete an original photo from your Photos library or a copy held by another service.

Photos and visual searches

Threadlook only sends a photo after you choose or take one and start a visual search. The image and selected crop are sent over HTTPS to the Threadlook search backend. The backend processes the image in memory and sends it to SerpApi’s Image API so the app can request Google Lens product matches. Threadlook does not intentionally save search images to backend disk storage.

SerpApi’s current Image API documentation says an uploaded image ID expires after 10 minutes. SerpApi states that search data is retained for up to 31 days unless its ZeroTrace option is used; Threadlook’s current integration does not enable ZeroTrace. SerpApi is based in the United States, so image-search data may be processed there. See SerpApi’s privacy and retention information for its current practices.

How information is used and shared

Search images and crop details are used to return product matches. Local favorites and search history power the features you use in the app. Threadlook does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in this project.

Search images are shared with Threadlook’s backend host and SerpApi for the search you requested. If you open a retailer link, that retailer receives your visit under its own privacy policy. If you email support, your email provider processes the message you choose to send.

Retention and requests

Threadlook keeps favorites and recent-search records on your device until you remove them, clear search history, or remove the app and its local data. The backend handles uploaded image data in memory for the request and does not intentionally persist the image to disk. SerpApi’s stated search-data retention is up to 31 days; uploaded image IDs expire after 10 minutes.

For a privacy question or a request for help with information related to a search, email krishna@threadlook.in. Do not include sensitive information unless it is needed to explain your request.

Children, changes, and contact

Threadlook is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review the request.

We may update this policy when the app or its service providers change. The current version will be available from Settings and from the public policy page linked in the App Store listing.
